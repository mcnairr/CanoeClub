Kansas City’s paddling club

Celebrating 70 years on the water.

We are a club of families and friends who float, hike, bike, and camp together across Missouri, Arkansas, and Kansas, and who have spent every year since 1960 hauling trash out of the rivers we love.

Why we exist

To explore and enjoy the wilderness waterways of the Ozarks, to share that experience with others, and to leave the streams better than we found them.

The club was organized in 1956 by families who had been canoeing together on Ozark streams and felt that someone ought to help keep those streams wild. That is still the whole idea. Trips are led by members, planned at our monthly meetings, and open to everyone from first-timers to lifelong paddlers.

  • Canoeing & kayaking

    Spring-fed Ozark floats, multi-day river camps, and easy day paddles close to home.

  • Hiking & backpacking

    Day hikes around the metro, like the Lawrence River Trail and our First Day of the Year hike at Kill Creek Park, and overnight trips into the Ozark hills.

  • Bicycling

    Group rides on rail-trails and quiet back roads.

  • Conservation

    Quarterly water-quality testing on the Blue River as Missouri Stream Team #41, and a river clean-up every year. More about our conservation work

  • Campfires & potlucks

    A potluck dinner before every meeting, Dutch oven cooking, and long evenings around the fire on river trips.

A year with the club

There is always something on the calendar.

Winter

Plans and programs

The year opens with a First Day of the Year hike on January 1. Members submit their trips for the new calendar by mid-January, and winter Stream Team testing on the Blue River carries on in the cold.

Spring

High water, first floats

Ozark streams run full and clear. Weekend floats and campouts start, along with early-season hikes before the leaves close in.

Summer

Long floats, warm camps

Multi-day river trips, family campouts, and lazy afternoons on gravel bars, with day paddles and hikes close to home.

Fall

The clean-up and Canoe Fest

The annual clean-up weekend in September, when we fill canoes with whatever doesn’t belong in the river, then the week-long Fall Canoe Fest on the Current in October, with bluffs lit up in color.

Meet us in person

Second Saturday, every month

Visitors are always welcome. No boat required.

When
The second Saturday of the month. Potluck dinner at 6:30 p.m., business meeting 7:30 to 8:30 p.m.
Where
Camp Lake of the Woods dining hall, 5700 Oakwood Rd., Swope Park, Kansas City, Missouri
Cost
Free. Bring a dish to share if you like, or just show up and say hello.

Not every month has a meeting in the hall: when a club trip falls on the second Saturday, the meeting sometimes moves to the campfire. Check the current calendar before your first visit.

What a meeting looks like

  • A potluck dinner to start the evening
  • Upcoming trips announced and sign-ups taken
  • Reports and photos from recent trips
  • A short program: a river, a destination, a skill, or a guest speaker
  • Conservation news and clean-up planning
  • Time to meet the people you will be floating with

Join the club

Membership is simple and inexpensive.

Dues cover a family or an individual for the calendar year. Members get the monthly newsletter and the club calendar by email, plus access to the members-only archive of past newsletters, schedules, and forms. The easiest way to join is to come to a meeting; you can also join on any trip. Read the latest newsletter.
